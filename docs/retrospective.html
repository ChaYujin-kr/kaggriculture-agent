Kaggriculture Retrospective

Ten days, from a scratch planner rated 437 to a pair of tuned public forks, with one expensive detour in between. The companion page, Kaggriculture Field Notes, explains the game, the scoring and the tools, and replays three highlight games. This page covers what happened, in order, and what we would keep.

Glossary · 12 terms used on this page
Agent
The program that plays the game. One Python file per agent.
Submission, slot
Uploading an agent. Only the latest 2 (the active slots) keep playing; a new one retires the older.
Ladder, rating
The live table where agents play each other; the rating rises on wins and falls on losses. New agents start near 600.
Fork, lineage
A fork is a modified copy of someone's agent. Forks of one ancestor form a lineage.
Tape
A recorded sequence of actions. Most strong agents replay tapes and adjust a few decisions.
Seed, seat
The seed fixes the game's random events; the seat is whether an agent plays first or second.
Gauntlet, wall
A test pool weighted by the ladder's lineage mix; a wall is a lineage a candidate must beat.
Knob
A tunable constant inside an agent's code, such as _V92_P_TOP.
Hold-out
Games kept aside while choosing, used only to confirm the choice.
Commit
One saved change in the project's git history. The grey codes (like aabe21e) point to them.
Dry-run
Running the daily pipeline without actually submitting.
fastsim
Our headless copy of the game engine that reproduces ladder games exactly.

The log

Day by day

21 SepMon

Our own planner, then the public baselines

We started with a market-aware tile planner and a greedy task scheduler of our own aabe21e, plus a fast simulator and an evolution-strategy tuner 112fca9. v1 and v2 rated 437 and 499. The unmodified public 2945 Farm, submitted as a baseline, rated 2065. A fertilizer gap-filling layer on a tape agent gained nothing and was kept only for the record 0fbe804.

22 SepTue

The best rating we ever held

The public 2965 Master Hybrid rated 2369 unmodified. A search over eight of its exposed constants won 14/16 against the unmodified agent and took it to 2644.5, about 200th on the board.

23 SepWed

v7, the pool mistake, and the pivot

The flag search was validated 45e9a59 and wrapped into a daily refresh: pull new public agents, duel the champion, submit if better a37ffff. It picked aurax7's v7, which had beaten everything in our local pool, and we tuned its late-season gates 02afdda. On the ladder, v7 and v7_endgame rated about 1650, a thousand below hybrid. Our pool had no V52/V53 and no herd-safe agents, and V53 beats v7 in 93% of games.

The same day we pivoted to a "zero to one" effort: four competing tracks to build an agent of our own (top-down rules, imitation of strong agents, whole-game RL, and a zoo of off-meta farms). The tracks wrote proposals, criticised each other's, revised, and audited 219k episodes from the community dataset. None reached a playable agent before the deadline. Our scratch planner banked 73k solo against 170k for the champion, and the gap was execution: 83 CARE actions against 410, and 60 fertilizer collections against 372.

24 SepThu

A PowerShell-written file with a byte-order mark crashed the daily refresh before it did anything a33f292.

25 SepFri

Measure the population you will actually meet

Twelve commits. The overnight refresh had auto-submitted the Shepherd's Ledger, which beat v7 6/6 locally, so the refresh went to dry-run. We moved the lineage tools into the repo 8d5a604, measured the opponent mix in our target band 0c988ba, and built a lineage-weighted gauntlet with walls that a candidate must hold against every agent 316ad9a 7b637e9. Daily submissions now had to pass it a7ecb82. Splitting v7's bulk endgame sales made it worse, which ruled out that explanation of its losses e0e6b45. Replaying our own ladder games on their real seeds showed fastsim matches the ladder to the coin fc50edc. We also resubmitted hybrid to re-measure it on the current ladder; it settled at 1830.

26 SepSat

We re-read the official Evaluation, Timeline and Rules pages. Only the latest two submissions count, so every new submission retires one. A gauntlet run killed for low memory lost all its games, so results are now saved as they finish 35ad462. Kaggle's top-episode dumps showed the top 23 teams open unlike any public lineage.

27 SepSun

One constant that mattered

_V92_P_TOP, the number of predicted opponent sales the agent front-runs, went from 2 to 6: the Shepherd's Ledger rose from 81.2% to 95.4% on the gauntlet 6d8e45a. We submitted shepherd_p6 and hybrid_cxd_p8 as the active pair.

28 SepMon

Tapes of real opponents

The gauntlet said our agents beat the cha22 lineage. The ladder said cha22 beat the Shepherd's Ledger in 72% of 93 games, because its ladder versions differ from every public file by day 2. So we replayed candidates against open-loop tapes of the actual opponents on our actual boards 168e667. On 195 boards the played files reproduced 195/195. A public re-upload of harvest-ledger (ttv1) won 141 of them against 119 for shepherd_p6, though the gauntlet ranked it lower. We submitted it 28ee0ee and pinned the engine to 1.32.7 a76bf5f.

29 SepTue

Near-level boards and a knob screen

We built tapes from the community dataset: 188 games by 117 teams rated 2300–2399. _V92_P_TOP 6 against 8 made no difference; ttv1 beat the Shepherd's Ledger 133 to 118. A 16-knob screen found one real lever: selling ahead of the agent's own plan (_S738_LOOK). Entering tomatoes more readily cost 16 wins. The daily refresh learned to spot re-run kernels by content hash 63b0a88 ee39b96.

30 SepWed · deadline

Hold-out, then the last submission

The session-only schedule meant to run the check at 00:30 UTC died with a session restart, so the work started at 05:20 instead. On 152 boards that had played no part in the choice, the _S738_LOOK = 10 variant won 9 more and lost none; over all 340 boards, +15/−0. We submitted it at 07:50 UTC as the second half of the final pair 563c8bb.

What we keep

Lessons

  1. Evaluate against the population you will face

    A pool missing the ladder's main lineages picked v7 and cost about 1,000 rating points. Fingerprint what the ladder actually serves, weight by it, and treat the lineages that beat you as walls.

  2. Local copies of an opponent are not the opponent

    Public files of a lineage can be far weaker than its ladder versions. Tapes of the real games were the only evaluation that matched the ladder's verdict.

  3. Reproduce before you trust

    Every evaluation began by checking that the file that played reproduces the ladder's banks exactly. When that holds, a disagreement between candidates is real.

  4. Pair everything and count the noise

    Margins swing about $9k a game and outcomes cluster by seed. Same seeds, both seats, at least six seeds, and a reason to distrust any gain found in 40 games.

  5. Choose on one set, confirm on another

    _S738_LOOK was picked on 188 boards and confirmed on 152 new ones. A gain that survives unseen boards is worth a submission slot.

  6. A constant is dead until a board changes

    Many exposed knobs never change a single game (_V92_Q_* in ttv1). Screen for "boards changed" before tuning, and stop at saturation: _V92_P_TOP helped from 2 to 6 and did nothing after.

  7. Automation near a slot rule needs a brake

    Only two submissions stay active, so an unattended auto-submit retires a keeper. The daily refresh went to dry-run and a gauntlet gate. Anything scheduled must also survive a restart; ours did not.

What we would do differently

Build the tape evaluation on day one instead of day eight. Keep the from-scratch effort to one track with a hard gate, since the gap was execution quality and four parallel designs did not close it in a week. Keep one slot for a different lineage only if tapes show it covers a real weakness.

배운 것

다음에도 기억할 것

  1. 진짜 만날 상대로 연습하기

    연습 상대가 실제 대회와 다르면, 연습에서 1등이어도 실전에서 질 수 있어요. v7이 그랬어요. 레이팅 1,000점을 잃었죠.

  2. 복사본은 진짜 상대가 아니에요

    공개된 상대 에이전트는 래더의 진짜 상대보다 훨씬 약할 수 있어요. 실제 경기를 녹화한 테이프만 래더와 같은 결과를 냈어요.

  3. 먼저 똑같이 나오는지 확인하기

    실제로 낸 에이전트로 다시 돌렸을 때 래더 결과가 그대로 나오는지부터 확인했어요. 그게 맞아야 비교 결과를 믿을 수 있어요.

  4. 운을 조심하기

    한 판 결과는 운 때문에 9천 달러씩 오르내려요. 그래서 같은 시드, 양쪽 자리, 적어도 시드 6개로 비교해요. 40판으로 찾은 개선은 일단 의심해요.

  5. 고르는 시험과 확인하는 시험을 나누기

    _S738_LOOK은 188판으로 고르고, 처음 보는 152판으로 확인했어요. 새 문제에서도 맞아야 슬롯을 쓸 가치가 있어요.

  6. 돌려도 아무 일 없는 노브도 많아요

    어떤 노브는 바꿔도 한 판도 결과가 안 바뀌어요(ttv1의 _V92_Q_*). 그리고 좋아지다가 멈추는 지점이 있어요. _V92_P_TOP은 2에서 6까지는 도움이 됐지만, 그 뒤로는 그대로였어요.

  7. 자동 도구에는 브레이크를

    슬롯이 2개뿐이라, 자동 제출이 아끼던 에이전트를 빼 버릴 수 있어요. 그래서 드라이런으로 바꾸고 게이틀릿을 통과해야만 내게 했어요. 예약한 일도 프로그램이 다시 시작되면 사라질 수 있다는 걸 배웠어요.

다시 한다면

실제 상대 테이프로 시험하는 방법을 8일째가 아니라 첫날부터 만들 거예요. 우리만의 에이전트 만들기는 네 팀으로 나누지 않고, 한 팀에 집중하고 분명한 합격 기준을 둘 거예요. 부족했던 건 아이디어가 아니라 일을 꼼꼼히 해내는 힘이었거든요. 두 슬롯 중 하나를 다른 계열로 채우는 건, 테이프 시험에서 정말로 약점을 막아 준다고 나올 때만 할 거예요.
