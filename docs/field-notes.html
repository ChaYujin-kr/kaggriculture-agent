Glossary · 14 terms used on this page
Agent
The program that plays the game. One Python file per agent.
Submission, slot
Uploading an agent. Up to 5 a day, but only the latest 2 (the active slots) keep playing.
Ladder, rating
The live table where active agents play each other automatically. The rating moves up on a win and down on a loss; new agents start near 600.
Bradley-Terry
A model that estimates each agent's strength from who beat whom. One fit over the post-deadline games sets the final order.
Fork, lineage
A fork is a copy of someone's agent with changes. Forks of one ancestor form a lineage.
Tape, tape agent
A recorded sequence of actions. A tape agent replays recorded routes and adjusts a few things (sales, herd, hires) to the game.
Opening fingerprint
An md5 over an agent's actions on turns 1–48, used to tell lineages apart.
Seed, seat
The seed fixes the game's random events, such as which shops open. The seat is whether an agent plays first or second.
Paired evaluation
Two candidates play the same seeds from the same seats, so luck cancels out of the comparison.
Gauntlet, wall
A test pool of agents from several lineages, weighted by how often the ladder serves them. A wall is a lineage a candidate must beat to pass.
Band
A rating range, such as teams rated 2300–2399.
Knob
A tunable constant inside an agent's code, such as _S738_LOOK.
Hold-out
Games kept aside while choosing, then used only to confirm the choice.
fastsim
Our headless copy of the official game engine that reproduces ladder games exactly.

The game

A 30-day farm, a shared market, one winner

Two agents run neighbouring farms for 720 turns: 30 days of 24 hours. Each turn they move farmers and hired hands, plant wheat, carrot, tomato, melon and strawberry, keep cows, sheep, geese and chickens, and trade on one market that both farms share. Whoever has more coins in the bank after turn 720 wins. The size of the win does not matter.

  • The market is a set of small reservoirs. Selling pushes a product's price down for both players, and town shops drain stock back out. Melon has no shop demand, so about 160 units drive it to the floor.
  • Shops unlock at random. Knowing the first two shops predicts the third with 13.4% accuracy against 12.5% for a random guess. Agents can react to shops but not anticipate them.
  • Labour is cheap. The n-th hire of a day costs fib(n), so about ten hands cost $143 a day. Hands vanish at the end of each day.
  • Step 718 is the last step whose actions run, so everything has to be dropped and sold before it.
  • The ladder engine is kaggle-environments 1.32.7, since 15 Aug. That version changed scarcity pricing for carrot, tomato and egg, so every local match must use it.
Scoring

How the final leaderboard is made

  • Each team may submit up to 5 agents a day. Only the latest two stay active, so every new submission retires the older of the pair.
  • Active agents play rated games against agents of similar rating. The rating moves on win, loss or tie only. A new agent starts near 600.
  • After the 30 Sep deadline, games keep running for about two weeks. One Bradley-Terry fit over those games then sets the final order.
  • Kaggle staff said on the forum that the fit uses every game played between agents that are still active. The live rating number itself is not carried over. Submitting earlier buys more games and a smaller error band, not a higher expected score.
The ladder

A population of forks

Almost every strong public agent is a tape agent: a library of recorded 720-turn routes plus reactive layers that adjust sales, herd and hires. Authors fork each other, so the ladder is a set of lineages rather than independent designs. We named lineages by an opening fingerprint. The fingerprint does not depend on the seed but does depend on the opponent, so each agent is fingerprinted against several.

Lineages form a rock-paper-scissors cycle: v7 beats hybrid, hybrid beats V53, and V53 beats v7. It reproduced 8/8 each way locally. The top 23 teams in Kaggle's own episode dumps all open differently from every public lineage, so the top of the board runs private agents.

Method

Five tools, in the order we came to trust them

  1. fastsim: a headless match runner

    It runs the official interpreter about 20× faster and reproduces ladder games to the coin. Replaying 391 of our ladder games, six opening groups came out exact, including 29 v7 games against six different teams. The misses were against near-forks of the files we had. Without that check, no local number means anything.

  2. Paired evaluation on fixed seeds

    Every candidate plays the same seeds from both seats as its baseline. Per-game margin sd is about $9k and outcomes cluster by seed, so 40-game trials "found" gains that reversed at 80. Six seeds is our minimum.

  3. A lineage-weighted gauntlet

    It plays two agents per lineage, weighted by the ladder mix, with walls that a candidate must hold against every agent. It replaced picking by head-to-head wins against one champion, which is how a locally unbeaten v7 went to the ladder and lost 1,000 rating points.

  4. Tape evaluation on real ladder boards

    We turn the opponent seat of each of our ladder games into an open-loop tape and replay candidates on that game's seed and seat. The file that actually played gives back the ladder banks exactly (195/195, then 73/73 on new boards). This mattered most, because local copies of some lineages were far weaker than the versions on the ladder: the cha22 family lost every local game to the Shepherd's Ledger but won 72% of 93 real ones. Later we built tapes from other teams' games in the community dataset, 188 boards from 117 teams rated 2300–2399.

  5. Knob screens with a hold-out

    Public agents expose dozens of module constants. We screen one change at a time against the same boards, pick on one set, and confirm on boards that played no part in the choice.

Limit of tapes.

A tape repeats what the opponent did and cannot react to us, except through market prices. Absolute win counts are an upper bound. Only the comparison between candidates on the same tapes is reliable.

하이라이트

다시 봐도 짜릿한 세 경기

왼쪽이 우리 농장, 오른쪽이 상대 농장이에요. 아래 막대는 누가 돈을 얼마나 더 가졌는지 보여 줘요. 초록이면 우리가 앞선 거고, 갈색이면 뒤진 거예요. 그 밑의 선은 게임 전체 동안 돈 차이가 어떻게 바뀌었는지예요. 1~26일은 빠르게, 마지막 3일은 천천히 흘러가요.

게임

30일 농장, 함께 쓰는 시장, 승자는 한 명

두 에이전트가 이웃한 농장을 하나씩 맡아요. 게임은 720차례(30일 × 24시간)예요. 차례마다 농부와 일꾼을 움직여 밀·당근·토마토·멜론·딸기를 심고, 소·양·거위·닭을 키우고, 두 농장이 같이 쓰는 시장에 팔아요. 마지막에 은행에 돈이 더 많은 쪽이 이겨요. 얼마나 크게 이겼는지는 상관없어요. 1달러 차이도 이긴 거예요.

  • 시장은 작은 물통 같아요. 한 가지를 많이 팔면 값이 뚝 떨어지고, 그 값은 상대에게도 똑같이 적용돼요. 멜론은 가게에서 사 가지 않아서 160개쯤 팔면 거의 바닥 값이 돼요.
  • 가게는 무작위로 열려요. 처음 두 가게를 보고 세 번째를 맞힐 확률이 13.4%인데, 그냥 찍어도 12.5%예요. 그래서 미리 예측은 못 하고, 열린 다음에 반응만 할 수 있어요.
  • 일꾼은 싸요. 하루에 열 명쯤 써도 143달러예요. 일꾼은 그날 밤이 되면 사라져요.
  • 718번째 차례가 마지막으로 행동이 적용되는 차례예요. 그 전에 모두 팔아야 해요.
  • 대회는 kaggle-environments 1.32.7 엔진을 써요. 이 버전에서 당근·토마토·달걀 값 계산이 바뀌어서, 연습할 때도 꼭 같은 버전을 써야 해요.
순위

최종 순위는 이렇게 정해져요

  • 하루에 5번까지 제출할 수 있지만, 활성 슬롯은 가장 최근 2개뿐이에요. 새로 제출하면 둘 중 오래된 쪽이 빠져요.
  • 활성 에이전트는 래더에서 레이팅이 비슷한 상대와 자동으로 경기해요. 이기면 레이팅이 오르고 지면 내려가요. 새 에이전트는 600점쯤에서 시작해요.
  • 9월 30일 마감 뒤에도 2주쯤 경기를 계속해요. 그다음 그 경기 기록 전체로 브래들리-테리 계산을 한 번 해서 최종 순위를 정해요.
  • 그래서 지금 레이팅 숫자가 그대로 최종 점수가 되지는 않아요. 일찍 제출하면 경기 기록이 많아져서 결과가 더 정확해질 뿐, 점수가 더 높아지지는 않아요.
래더

포크들의 가족 모임

방법

우리가 믿게 된 도구 다섯 가지

  1. fastsim: 빠른 연습 경기장

    대회와 똑같은 게임을 내 컴퓨터에서 20배쯤 빠르게 돌려요. 우리 래더 경기 391판을 다시 돌려 봤더니, 여섯 개 오프닝 그룹은 동전 한 닢까지 똑같이 나왔어요. 이게 맞아야 연습 결과를 믿을 수 있어요.

  2. 짝 비교

    두 후보를 똑같은 시드에서, 양쪽 자리 모두 둬 보고 비교해요. 한 판 결과는 운 때문에 9천 달러씩 오르내려요. 그래서 40판만 보고 "좋아졌다"고 믿었다가 80판에서 뒤집힌 적도 있어요. 시드는 적어도 6개를 써요.

  3. 계열별 게이틀릿

    래더에 실제로 많은 계열들을, 만나는 비율대로 모아 시험을 봐요. 벽인 계열은 꼭 이겨야 통과예요. 예전에는 우리 연습 상대만 이기는 v7을 골랐다가, 래더에서 레이팅 1,000점을 잃었거든요.

  4. 실제 상대의 테이프로 시험하기

    가장 중요한 도구였어요. 우리가 실제로 둔 래더 경기에서 상대가 한 행동을 테이프로 만들고, 같은 시드와 자리에서 새 후보와 붙여 봐요. 실제로 냈던 에이전트로 돌리면 결과가 진짜 경기와 똑같이 나와요(195판 중 195판, 새 경기 73판 중 73판). 이게 필요했던 이유가 있어요. cha22 계열의 공개 복사본은 연습에서 양치기 장부에게 전부 졌는데, 진짜 래더에서는 93판 중 72%를 이겼거든요. 나중에는 다른 팀들 경기로도 테이프를 만들었어요. 2300점대 117팀의 188판이에요.

  5. 노브 하나씩 바꿔 보기, 그리고 홀드아웃

    공개 에이전트에는 노브가 수십 개 있어요. 하나씩 바꿔서 같은 경기들로 비교하고, 한 묶음으로 고른 다음, 고를 때 쓰지 않은 홀드아웃 경기로 한 번 더 확인해요.

테이프의 한계
